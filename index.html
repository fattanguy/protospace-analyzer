    What this evidence can establish

    Quantitative = Σ(metric weight × normalized metric). Qualitative = mean of two automatic 0–4 interpretations × 25. Combined = 0.70 × quantitative + 0.30 × qualitative, rounded once. Missing evidence is left unscored, not assigned zero. The 70/30 split is a proposed project preference.

    Bands: 0–19 very limited, 20–39 limited, 40–59 moderate, 60–79 strong, 80–100 very strong fit. Fit describes this rubric's intent, not design success. A high score for every descriptor is not the goal.

      The selected area can be modelled in the 3D generator above: its cells become a relief at the depths the tones imply, the frame image is projected onto it, and the cut A–A becomes a live section plane you can orbit around.

      Reconstruction assumptions. A single render cannot recover the real interior. The section places visible mass at estimated depths of 0, 5.5, 13.5 or 22 m from the tone bands, then gives it the solid depth you choose; the 3D generator can additionally read the tone continuously (0–26 m) for a smoother relief. Unmodeled space is not a verified room or opening. Level lines are reference datums, not detected floors.

      How the image is read

      The cube lattice gives the scale — 1 cube = 1 m — and every metre of the face is classed by its tone, which in these renders tracks how deep the first cube sits: black at the front, mid grey a few metres back, light grey deep in a recess, white where you see straight through. Modules are 9 × 9 m, floors 3 m; the module grid is fitted where the full/empty reading is clearest.

      Rubric v3: quantitative scores are the sum of each normalized metric multiplied by its displayed weight. A score of 100 means every target is met. These targets are inherited project assumptions, not universal architectural standards; they have not been independently validated. Recognition compares the import with all 250 frames.

      Quantitative and qualitative ratings

      The quantitative rating measures geometry visible in the image. The qualitative rating automatically interprets two visible spatial patterns for each descriptor and generates a written explanation. It uses local rules applied to the same measurements, so it is not an independent second opinion or a vision-language AI review. It cannot verify actual use, comfort or behavior.

      Each automatic criterion combines the normalized metrics named in its explanation: minimum for patterns requiring all features, mean for a single feature, or geometric mean for the balanced neighborhood mix. Strength below 0.2 scores 0; 0.2–below 0.4 scores 1; 0.4–below 0.6 scores 2; 0.6–below 0.8 scores 3; 0.8–1 scores 4. The mean is multiplied by 25 to produce a score out of 100.
