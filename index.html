    How the frame is read

    The frame is looked at from the front. Every metre of the face is classed by how deep its first cube sits: black at the front, grey a few metres back, light grey deep inside, white where you see straight through. Modules are 9 m wide and 3 m tall; the module grid is placed where the full/empty reading is clearest. 1 cube = 1 m.

    Scores describe fit to this project's targets, not architectural quality. They are a tool for comparing frames, not a verdict.

    • first mass 3–8 m back
